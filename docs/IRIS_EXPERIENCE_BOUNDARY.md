# IRIS Experience Boundary

## Purpose

IRIS has one underlying governed system and two intentionally distinct user experiences.

They are connected, but they do not expose the same information or serve the same purpose.

## 1. Financial Life / Results experience

This is the user-specific product experience.

It is where IRIS may present, when actually supported by governed evidence and runtime state:

- the user's observed financial reality;
- user-specific financial state;
- changes, relationships, patterns, and supported findings;
- scenarios, decisions, consequences, and outcomes where implemented and evidence-qualified;
- the user report inventory;
- report activation/deactivation and other product controls; and
- detailed explanations of the results IRIS produces from the user's governed financial reality.

The report universe is intentionally massive. Reports may describe observed reality directly or express intelligence-derived results. They must remain epistemically distinguishable.

## 2. IRIS Intelligence experience

The Intelligence experience is currently read-only and educational.

It must not load or display user financial records, user-specific balances, user transactions, user-specific intelligence results, or user-specific report results.

Its purpose is to teach and explain the IRIS intelligence system itself, including:

- the eight authoritative architectural domains;
- the current seven-domain executable Sandbox boundary and the deferred Statements domain;
- evidence states and evidence gates;
- canonical financial state as an architectural concept;
- interpretation and analytical transformations;
- temporal, statistical, relational, and behavioral reasoning;
- patterns and anomaly reasoning;
- causal boundaries;
- prediction and uncertainty;
- scenarios and counterfactuals;
- risk and opportunity reasoning;
- decision and consequence reasoning;
- outcomes and learning;
- cross-domain synthesis;
- higher-order intelligence;
- arbitrary derived-intelligence nodes;
- recursive composition;
- graph traversal and reverse lineage; and
- the distinction between semantic depth and runtime/materialization limits.

No user data is necessary to teach these concepts.

## One connected system

The two experiences are not two products and must not become two independent architectures.

They share one governed conceptual and runtime foundation:

`provider evidence → governed evidence boundary → canonical state → intelligence graph → intelligence results → user-facing results/reports`

The Financial Life / Results experience exposes user-specific reality and results.

The Intelligence experience explains the reasoning architecture that transforms governed reality into intelligence.

The connection is bidirectional at the system level:

`financial reality → intelligence → result`

and

`result/intelligence concept → reasoning lineage → governed reality/evidence`

The educational Intelligence surface does not need to expose a user's actual lineage to explain that architecture.

## Report boundary

Reports belong to the Financial Life / Results product experience, not to the educational Intelligence surface.

The Intelligence surface may explain that reports are downstream expressions of intelligence, but it must not become a report catalog, report dashboard, or user-specific intelligence-results dashboard.

## Preservation rule

Existing intelligence architecture must not be weakened merely to create the experience split.

The split is a presentation/product boundary, not a reduction of intelligence capability.

Arbitrary derived intelligence, recursive lineage, higher-order composition, cross-domain synthesis, causal/predictive/scenario reasoning where supported, and future recursive extension remain part of the underlying IRIS intelligence architecture.

## Current implementation rule

When an existing surface mixes user data, report products, and intelligence education, classify each element before changing it:

1. User-specific reality/result → Financial Life / Results.
2. User-specific report/product → Financial Life / Results.
3. User-specific intelligence result → Financial Life / Results.
4. Generic explanation of intelligence architecture → educational Intelligence.
5. Provider/evidence inspection for the user's actual connection → Financial Life / Results / Evidence.
6. Internal execution machinery → backend/runtime, not a user-facing hierarchy level.

Nothing should be moved or deleted solely by visual appearance; the actual data dependency and semantic role determine the destination.

## Current Sandbox statement

The architecture has eight authoritative domains. The present executable Sandbox boundary is seven domains: Authentication, Transactions, Balance, Identity, Assets, Liabilities, and Investments.

Statements is Domain 8 and remains deliberately deferred until real banking. It must not be simulated, presented as observed, or required for current Sandbox intelligence execution.
