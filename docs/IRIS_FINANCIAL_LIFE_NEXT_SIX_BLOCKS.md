# IRIS Financial Life — Next Six Priority 1 Product Blocks

These six blocks continue the Financial Life / Results build after the foundational six. They are product-experience boundaries inside one IRIS system, not intelligence-depth limits, report-count limits, or separate products.

## Block 7 — Product Discovery

Purpose: help a person discover the expanding report universe without confusing the catalog with produced financial results.

Required behavior:
- browse the registered product universe;
- search by name, purpose, family, output type and declared evidence requirements;
- navigate by family;
- expose product identity, purpose and intended output;
- distinguish definition-only products from products with actual runtime output;
- never imply that catalog presence means user-specific availability or production.

## Block 8 — Product Qualification & Publication State

Purpose: show what is actually possible for this user and why.

Required behavior:
- independently represent definition, evidence qualification, intelligence qualification, runtime production, certification, activation and publication;
- show limited, unavailable and deferred states explicitly;
- explain missing prerequisites without inventing values;
- use actual governed runtime records for runtime/certification state;
- preserve the distinction between provider availability and user authorization.

## Block 9 — Report Context & Deep Detail

Purpose: turn an individual report product into a complete exploration surface.

Required behavior:
- explain what the product is for;
- show declared evidence requirements and dependencies;
- show the strongest defensible product title;
- expose the exact runtime result only when one exists;
- expose limitations and epistemic state;
- provide a route back to the library and forward into related products, reasoning and evidence.

## Block 10 — Relationship & Related-Product Exploration

Purpose: let users move sideways through connected financial meaning rather than treating every report as an isolated artifact.

Required behavior:
- identify related products using governed catalog relationships and declared dependencies;
- expose shared evidence domains and feature mappings without presenting them as proof of execution;
- support report-to-report exploration;
- preserve the selected product/context while navigating;
- eventually connect exact runtime intelligence relationships when certified lineage exists.

## Block 11 — Question-Driven Discovery

Purpose: make the catalog respond to what the person wants to understand, not only to predefined navigation labels.

Required behavior:
- accept natural-language discovery terms;
- map questions to relevant registered products without claiming a result exists;
- show why a product is relevant using its declared purpose/dependencies;
- distinguish "a product may answer this" from "IRIS has answered this";
- route qualified questions into actual runtime results when evidence and intelligence permit.

Question-driven discovery is a discovery mechanism, not permission to fabricate an answer.

## Block 12 — Return, Continuity & User Control

Purpose: make the Financial Life experience continuous rather than a collection of disconnected screens.

Required behavior:
- preserve meaningful navigation context;
- return users from report → reasoning → evidence → related report without losing the original context;
- preserve user report activation choices independently of provider consent and intelligence execution;
- provide clear paths back to Life, Change, Understand, Evidence and Reports;
- keep the persistent Financial Life assistant available;
- support future outcomes/learning without implying that an action or outcome occurred when it was not observed.

## Six-block product flow

```text
Discover
  ↓
Qualify
  ↓
Understand a product
  ↓
Explore relationships
  ↓
Ask what you want to know
  ↓
Return with continuity and control
```

## Governing invariant

The six blocks expand the user's ability to discover and navigate IRIS products without expanding the truth boundary. A product definition may exist without Plaid user data; a user-specific factual result requires the applicable governed evidence, intelligence, lineage and publication gates.

The report universe remains conceptually unbounded. These blocks define how a person navigates that universe; they do not define how many products IRIS can produce.

## Certification

Implementation status must remain separate from certification. The applicable chain is:

`Architecture Defined → Contract Defined → Schema Implemented → Runtime Implemented → Independently Executable → Evidence Verified → Exact Evidence Boundary Verified → Semantic Lineage Verified → Report Product Defined → Report Product User-Controlled → Report Product Surfaced → Interaction Verified → Deployment Verified → End-to-End Certified`
